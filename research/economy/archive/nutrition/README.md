# Archive: nutrition notes, models and tools up to M103

Moved here in M104 (R56). They are history, not a basis.

**The basis now:** `research/economy/nutrition_record.md`. It holds only your inputs and the facts verified in named sources.

**Why they were archived:** these files contain errors you corrected, among them:
- a nutrient score scaled wrongly;
- satiety counted in several ways;
- a well-being formula that dropped reward and lightness;
- fixed water and nutrient needs;
- an unsourced fish composition that made a fishing village starve;
- tie-breaks based on stock size;
- a misreading of the hydration index.

They also contain estimates that were never sourced.

**Contents:**
- `round_m82.md` to `round_m96.md`: the rounds on nutrition, food, energy and health display.
- `food_model_spec.md`, `food_model_worked_example.md`, `food_model_assessment.md`, `pdf/`: food model versions 1-4.
- `food_resources.md`: the food survey (R21).
- `tools/`, `data/satiety_fit.json`: the model and fitting code.

**What still stands from the m84-m88 rounds** (recorded in `design.json`, decision D30), because they are about health and settlement rather than nutrition:
- the size curve;
- yearly steps;
- the food box;
- the health faces;
- density and sanitation;
- the market buildings.

The resource records of the game (XML) in `food_resources.md` section 1 are records and can be re-read from the branch.
