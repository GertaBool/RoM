3 · City artwork
A photo supplies surface color; it does not reconstruct architecture. The box route uses the image on the four walls and a separate roof texture. OBJ uses the mesh’s own UV layout. Imported models must be triangulated with one baked diffuse texture.
Generated façades work with box geometry. OBJ models keep your uploaded UV atlas. Detail is painted into the diffuse texture, so it survives export.
The complete image is stretched to a square atlas without cropping. UV positions remain aligned. Output is opaque DXT3 with mipmaps; no PBR, normal maps, animation or alpha-cutout materials.
The preview checks geometry and UV placement, not Civ IV lighting or city placement.
Using Blender, FBX, glTF or complex architectural scenes
Use the included prepare_building.py script with Blender to bake a single diffuse atlas and export a triangulated OBJ. The guide explains the command. The browser does not directly read FBX, GLB, BLEND or CAD files. CAD must first be tessellated into a mesh. Check the baked texture and remove interior geometry before import.
New meshes are centered, grounded and normalized to a two-unit horizontal span. Use scale to tune their city size. Only footprint names found in your mod’s city layout are offered.
